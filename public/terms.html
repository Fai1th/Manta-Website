Using Manta

You must meet the minimum age and other requirements in Discord’s Terms of Service. Follow those terms, Discord’s Community Guidelines, applicable law and the rules of your server.

Server administration

Server staff are responsible for their moderation decisions, the content they submit, enabled integrations and the permissions they give Manta. Review the target, reason and scope before taking an action. Keep private records and downloaded transcripts secure, and tell members how your server uses the bot.

Administrators can change Manta’s permissions or remove it from Discord. Removing it stops new Discord activity collection; deletion of stored records and backups is a separate step described in our privacy policy.
