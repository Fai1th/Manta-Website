A few things
worth knowing.

Is Manta free and open source?

Yes. Every included feature is free, with no Manta subscription, license key or premium tier. The bot and dashboard use the MIT License, so you can use, modify and redistribute the source with its license notice. Hosting and storage are separate and may cost money.

Does my computer have to stay on?

No. The bot and dashboard run together on your cloud host. Use an always-running container with persistent storage so events are tracked while you're away.

Can I manage everything from the website?

Daily operations and settings are managed through the dashboard. Creating the Discord application, granting its permissions, and configuring cloud secrets happen in Discord and your host.

What does Manta track?

Observed message counts, active days, voice presence, joins/leaves, and participation trends in eligible public channels. Bots, AFK channels and configured exclusions are omitted. Activity metrics do not store message text; support transcript exports are a separate staff action.

Can I add it to multiple servers?

This version is built for one server per deployed instance. You can deploy separate instances for separate servers, each with its own database and configuration.

Does it include leveling or minigames?

Manta focuses on server management and meaningful activity statistics. There are no RPG systems, XP economies or minigames.
